Delivery & Returns

Faith Over Fear delivers streetwear across Rwanda. We offer reliable delivery in Kigali and nationwide. Learn about our delivery schedule, shipping costs, and return policy for your F>F orders.

Delivering Info

  • Kigali city: All deliveries are done on wednesday and sunday (On a small transport fee).
  • In other provinces: Deliveries may be done also in other provinces using courier in public transport but on buyer`s risk.
  • Orders are processed within 1-2 business days delivered on wednesday and sunday.

Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love your gear. If something isn't right, we're here to fix it.

  • Visit our Contact Page to start a return.
  • Items must be in original, unworn condition with tags attached.
  • **Exchanges are free!** We want to get you the right size.
  • Returns for a full refund have a small 1000 FRW processing fee deducted to cover restocking.
  • Sale items are final sale.
