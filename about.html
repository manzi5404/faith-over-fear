Faith Over Fear.

We are the voice of the unheard. The strength of the struggle. The style of the spirit.

Faith Over Fear is a streetwear brand based in Kigali, Rwanda. F>F wasn't born in a boardroom. It was born in the quiet moments of doubt, where the only choice was to believe. We are a collective of dreamers, creators, and believers who refuse to let fear dictate our future. We don't just design streetwear; we design armor for the modern world.

Our Story

F>F started as a simple idea in 2024: what if your clothes could speak for you before you even said a word? What if a hoodie could be a shield?

Founded by three friends dealing with the pressures of modern life—anxiety, uncertainty, and the noise of the grind—we turned to creativity as our outlet. We wanted to wear our conviction. We wanted to represent a mindset that says, "No matter what's in front of me, my faith is greater."

From a garage setup to a global movement, F>F remains true to its roots: authentic, bold, and unashamed.
