Our story

Our conviction

Faith Over Fear isn't just a label; it's a declaration. Born in the streets of Rwanda and designed for the global movement, we bridge the gap between spiritual conviction and streetwear culture.

Every piece carries a message. Every stitch represents a stand. We don't just make clothing; we create armor for the bold, the believers, and the brave.

The quality

Every piece is engineered for longevity and comfort. From our 400GSM heavyweight hoodies to our precision-cut essential tees, we prioritize the fabric that carries our message.

Designed in Kigali. Crafted for the world. F>F represents more than fashion; it's a mindset that refuses to be silenced.

Delivery & returns

Faith Over Fear delivers streetwear across Rwanda. We offer reliable delivery in Kigali and nationwide.

Delivery info

  • Kigali city: All deliveries are done on Wednesday and Sunday (on a small transport fee).
  • Other provinces: Deliveries may also be done in other provinces using a courier in public transport, but at the buyer's risk.
  • Orders are processed within 1-2 business days and delivered on Wednesday and Sunday.
